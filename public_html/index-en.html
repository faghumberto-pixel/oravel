What changes

A predictable operation starts with the right information at the right time.

Anyone who has run an equipment operation knows the value of having control in hand. These are the four changes Oravel brings to your day to day.

Predictability

Equipment ready when the customer needs it

Maintenance planned on time and at the right hour-meter reading, so equipment is available and the team works without surprises.

In Oravel: the work order is opened automatically by hour meter or due date, with a checklist and an assigned technician.
Availability

The right part, at the right time

With the real balance in sight, purchases are planned and maintenance does not wait for parts.

In Oravel: the part used on a work order leaves inventory on the spot, and you see the real balance and what is running out.
Trust

Delivery and return with transparency

Everything recorded on the way out and back: a clear and fair relationship with the customer, with no arguments.

In Oravel: checkout and return with a checklist, before and after photos and digital signature.
View of the real cost

Every piece of equipment with its own history

You know how much each machine earns and how much it costs, and decide with confidence what to keep, renew or sell.

In Oravel: each piece of equipment has its own file: maintenance history, parts and accumulated cost.
Also on the platform: vehicle and driver logistics, accounts payable and receivable with bank slips, a customer portal and integrated WhatsApp communication.

Manifesto

Every operation deserves to run with clarity.

We believe every equipment operation can run with clarity, predictability and peace of mind.

We saw this from the inside. We ran an equipment operation for years. We know the silence of a machine idle on the job site, the part missing at the wrong time, the customer waiting "since yesterday" and the month-end close that would not add up.

And we learned one thing: organized operations do not depend on luck or heroes. They depend on having the right information, at the right time, in the hands of whoever decides.

That is why we believe that:

  • Whoever builds an operation deserves to see all of it, in numbers, and grow with confidence.
  • Control is not bureaucracy. It is what lets you decide without guessing.
  • A good system does not complicate things. It starts with what is a priority and grows with your operation.
  • Technology is only worth it if it shows results: more equipment available, planned purchases, a clear cost for each piece of equipment.
  • Decision makers need a conversation in their own language. The language of decision makers is results, not features.

We put the experience of people who have been on your side of the counter into simple modules, connected from the field to the office.

Our promise. You do not have to take our word for it. Use it for 30 days, with us by your side, and measure. If it does not make sense, you owe nothing.
